# 🌍 Energy Market Reports & Data

This repository provides a structured collection of **energy market reports**, **datasets**, and **statistical analyses** focused on **supply**, **demand**, and **price dynamics** for key commodities such as **crude oil**, **natural gas**, and **refined products**.

The data and insights compiled here support **market intelligence**, **strategic decision-making**, and **forecasting activities** across the global energy sector.

---

## 📚 Data Sources

This repository consolidates information from leading and trusted institutions, including:

- 🏛 **EIA** – U.S. Energy Information Administration  
- 🌐 **JODI** – Joint Organisations Data Initiative  

These sources provide reliable and timely data on energy production, consumption, trade flows, and market balances.

---

## 📂 Repository Structure

## US Petroleum Stocks

This folder focuses on analyzing U.S. petroleum stock levels, including crude oil and its derivatives.

**Data Origin:**  
Collected from the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA).  
Access the original data here:  
[U.S. Weekly Petroleum Stocks](https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/pet_stoc_wstk_dcu_nus_w.htm)

---

## US Fuels Demand

This folder focuses on analyzing U.S. petroleum demand for oil and fuels.

**Data Origin:**  
Collected from the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA).  
Original data available at:  
[U.S. Weekly Supplied Products](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/weekly/gasoline.php)

---

## US Propane Stocks

This folder is dedicated to analyzing U.S. propane stock levels, important for residential, commercial, and industrial energy usage.

**Data Origin:**  
Sourced from the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA).  
Original dataset:  
[U.S. Weekly Propane Stocks](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/weekly/propane.php)

---

## EIA Energy Outlook

This folder analyzes U.S. oil production and consumption trends, providing insights into supply-demand balances, price dynamics, and market stability.

**Data Origin:**  
Sourced from the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA).  
Access the official data here:  
[Short-Term Energy Outlook (STEO) - EIA](https://www.eia.gov/outlooks/steo/data.php)

---

## JODI Energy Databases

This folder studies oil and derivatives across multiple countries using the Joint Organizations Data Initiative (JODI) database. Focus is on trends in production, consumption, and stock levels for global market insights.

**Data Origin:**  
Collected from the JODI Oil World Database.  
Original data available at:  
[JODI Oil World Database](https://www.jodidata.org/gas/database/data-downloads.aspx)
